Privacy policy

Quizbloom

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what Quizbloom collects, why, and what you can do about it. Quizbloom is run by Zigmas Reisas (“we”). Contact: zigmasreisas@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name, from Sign in with Apple, Google or email sign-up. With Apple you can hide your real email.
  • Notes you upload: photos and PDFs you choose to turn into quizzes.
  • Study data: generated quizzes, your answers, progress, XP, streaks and your onboarding answers.
  • Purchase status: whether you have an active subscription. Payments are handled by Apple or Google Play; we never see your card details.

We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

  • To create quizzes from your notes and save your progress.
  • To schedule reviews and study reminders (reminders are local notifications on your device).
  • To check your subscription and give you access to paid features.
  • To answer you when you contact support.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores your account, quizzes, progress and uploaded notes.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads the notes you upload to write quiz questions. Notes are sent only when you create a quiz.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google Play handle subscriptions and receipts.

These providers process data on our behalf and only to provide their service.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, quizzes, progress and uploaded notes are deleted.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete account. You can also email us to access, correct or delete your data. If you live in the EU/EEA or UK you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Quizbloom is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app for significant changes.
