Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Quizbloom, then tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

I paid but Quizbloom still asks me to unlock.
Open the paywall and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you paid with.

How do I delete my account?
In the app open Settings → Delete account. Your account, quizzes, progress and uploaded notes are deleted permanently.

A quiz failed to create.
Try a sharper, well-lit photo of printed or clearly handwritten notes, or a smaller PDF.
