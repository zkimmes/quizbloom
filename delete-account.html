Delete your account

Quizbloom: Notes to Quiz, by Zigmas Reisas

In the app

  1. Open Quizbloom and sign in.
  2. On Home, tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you can't open the app, email zigmasreisas@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account (name, email, sign-in details), all your quizzes and questions, your study progress, XP and streaks, your onboarding answers, and every photo and PDF of notes you uploaded.

What is kept

Nothing from your account is kept. Subscription purchase records are held by Apple or Google Play under their own policies, and cancelling your subscription is done in your App Store or Google Play account settings; deleting your Quizbloom account does not cancel it.
